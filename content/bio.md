I am an M.S. student in Computer Engineering at the **University of California, San Diego**, where I also received my B.S. in Computer Engineering (2026).

I work on robots that perceive, plan, and act on their own, with a focus on getting algorithms to run on **real hardware**:

- **Autonomous robots** — At the [Existential Robotics Laboratory](https://erl.ucsd.edu/) with [Prof. Nikolay Atanasov](https://natanaso.github.io/), I work across the autonomy stack: building a quadrotor with onboard computing and model predictive control, developing mapping (occupancy grids and 3D point maps from depth cameras) and motion planning.
- **Swarm robotics** — At the [Active & Living Matter Lab](https://www.tzerhan.com/) with Prof. Tzer-Han Tan, I build robot swarms inspired by the self-organization of starfish embryos, including the robots' first remote-control hardware and the first hardware deployment of simulation-trained RL policies.

My broader interests span **model predictive control, motion planning, and sim-to-real transfer**: making robots that are fast, safe, and reliable outside of simulation.
